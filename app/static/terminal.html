Pair classification & models

Five-minute candles from your selected source. Net-profit labels. Chronological evaluation.

What qualifies a pair?

Positive hedge ratio, return correlation ≥ 0.40, cointegration FDR below 0.05, validation AUC above 0.50, and at least eight validation signals with positive average modeled net return. FDR covers the entire configured candidate universe. A rejected pair has no chart in the trading terminal.

What does the ML model predict?

Positive net P&L on a mean-reversion pair trade: signal at a completed five-minute close, entry at the next candle’s open, exit five candles later. Labels include both legs, 3 bps fees, 5 bps adverse slippage, and simulated borrowing. Labels crossing gaps or overnight are excluded. This label policy differs from live paper exits, which can trigger earlier.

30% formation / 30% train / 20% validation / 20% test, with a six-bar purge. Threshold selection uses validation only. Test metrics are informational and do not select pairs. Overlapping labels are dependent; validation mean returns are not a strategy backtest. Upstox models expire for new automatic entries after 24 hours. Local models stay frozen until you rescan so forward simulation can be evaluated. Restarting clears active models and disables entries; rescan to retrain.
